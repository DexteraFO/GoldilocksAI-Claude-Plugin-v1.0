# Public research protocol

## Source hierarchy

1. Company filings, regulatory records, company website, official announcements, investor materials, and executive bios for statements within their scope.
2. Dated independent reporting and reputable sector research for context and corroboration.
3. Commercial databases, aggregators, and search snippets as leads or attributed estimates, never as silent ground truth.

Seek a second source for decision-changing claims such as revenue, funding, acquisition, leadership, or growth. If sources conflict, show both, their dates and scopes, and the unresolved discrepancy. An acquisition announcement is not always evidence of closing. Distinguish announcement, signed transaction, and completed transaction.

## Candidate evidence ledger

For each candidate, capture: legal/brand identity, URL, sector, geography, revenue range and fiscal year, employee estimate/date, ownership/transaction status/date, growth figure and period, funding history if relevant, CEO/date, thesis signals, contradictory signals, source URLs, and unresolved items. Source references should identify the exact supporting page, not only a homepage.

Use an explicit as-of date. For every dynamic claim, prefer a currently available document and mention when the latest public figure is old. Label company claims as `Reported`, `Third-party estimate`, `Inferred`, or `Unknown`. Inferences must describe what source facts support them.

## Discovery and filtering

Start with the user's watchlist if supplied. If not, search by product niche, relevant customers/channel, geography, financial-size signals, and ownership. Capture reasons for excluding names so the shortlist is auditable. Do not pad to a target number when the market or public information cannot support it.

Hard gates should match the mandate. Examples: geography, transaction type, sector, disqualifying ownership, and minimum/maximum size when explicitly hard. Unknown status yields `Conditional: verify`, not a silent pass. An acquired target may be relevant as a comparable but does not belong among independent targets unless the mandate explicitly includes subsidiaries, carve-outs, or secondary interests.

## Public-only boundary

The screenshots contain fields called CRM, ground truth, and relationship strength. This plugin has none of those inputs. Render `Public signal / evidence note` instead of CRM. Render a mock path with dashed or labeled hypothetical links; use generic roles. When private sources are connected in a later version, add source provenance and permissions before treating any private signal as verified.
